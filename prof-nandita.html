Biography

Prof. Nandita Srivastava is a distinguished Indian solar physicist whose research has significantly advanced our understanding of solar eruptions, coronal mass ejections (CMEs), solar activity, and space weather.

Throughout her career she has combined observational research, international collaborations, scientific leadership, and mentorship, helping establish a strong and vibrant solar physics community in India.

Her work has contributed to understanding the origin, propagation, and geoeffectiveness of coronal mass ejections and their influence on the near-Earth space environment.

Beyond research, she has inspired generations of students and young scientists through teaching, supervision, workshops, and collaborative research programmes.
