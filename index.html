About the Symposium

This online half-day symposium celebrates the remarkable scientific journey of Prof. Nandita Srivastava on the occasion of her superannuation.

Bringing together colleagues, collaborators, students and researchers, the symposium highlights India's progress in solar physics and space weather research while looking toward the future of heliophysics.

The event celebrates decades of contributions to coronal mass ejections, solar eruptions, Sun–Earth connections, scientific leadership and mentorship.

Learn More

Scientific Themes

The symposium brings together researchers across multiple areas of solar and heliospheric science.

Solar Physics

Solar activity, eruptions and magnetic fields.

CMEs

Coronal mass ejections and their evolution.

Sun–Earth Connection

Understanding how solar activity affects Earth.

Space Weather

Forecasting and monitoring the space environment.

Honouring Prof. Nandita Srivastava

A distinguished solar physicist whose pioneering work on coronal mass ejections, solar activity and space weather has significantly shaped heliophysics research in India.

Beyond research, she has inspired generations of students through mentorship, collaboration and scientific leadership.
