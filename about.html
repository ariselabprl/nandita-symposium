About the Symposium

Celebrating Milestones in Solar and Space Weather Research in India

Honouring the scientific journey and lasting contributions of Prof. Nandita Srivastava

About the Symposium

This online symposium is being organized to celebrate the distinguished scientific career of Prof. Nandita Srivastava on the occasion of her superannuation.

The symposium will bring together researchers, collaborators, students, and members of the solar and space weather community to reflect on India's remarkable progress in solar physics and heliophysics over the past three decades.

It will also provide an opportunity to discuss future directions in solar observations, coronal mass ejection research, Sun–Earth connections, and space weather forecasting.

Symposium Objectives

Solar Physics

Advances in understanding the Sun and its activity.

Space Weather

Discuss recent developments in Sun–Earth interactions.

Scientific Legacy

Celebrate decades of impactful research and mentorship.

Community

Bring together colleagues, collaborators, and young researchers.

Why This Symposium?

Solar and space weather research in India has witnessed tremendous growth over the past three decades. Prof. Nandita Srivastava has played an important role in advancing our understanding of coronal mass ejections, solar eruptions, and their impact on Earth's space environment.

This symposium recognizes her scientific leadership, dedication to mentoring young researchers, and contributions to building a vibrant solar physics community in India.
